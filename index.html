A few useful answers

Before you choose.

How do I find the right watch for me?

Start with how you plan to wear it. Dress watches suit more formal occasions, Sport designs bring a bolder look, Celestial dials add subtle detail, and Statement pieces stand out. Use the filters to narrow your preferences, save favourites, or compare up to three watches side by side.

What delivery options can I choose?

Normal delivery costs £15, with an estimated delivery time of 3–5 working days. Express delivery costs £30, with an estimated delivery time of 1–2 working days. Select your preferred option at checkout; postage is shown separately before you place your order.

Can I add delivery instructions?

Yes. Enter your delivery address at checkout and use the optional delivery instructions box for details such as an apartment number, entry buzzer or where to ring the bell. Check your address and postcode carefully before placing your order.

Which payment methods are available?

Checkout offers card and digital-wallet options, and shows the full total before you confirm.

How can I check my order status?

Choose Track an order and enter the order number and email address used at checkout. You can view the recorded status, items and selected delivery method. Keep your order number from the confirmation screen so you can find your order again.

How should I care for my watch?

Wipe the case and bracelet gently with a soft, dry cloth. Keep leather straps away from moisture, and avoid solvents, strong magnets and sudden temperature changes. Before swimming, showering or adjusting a watch in water, check the manufacturer’s instructions and the water-resistance rating for that specific model.

How do I check sizing and watch specifications?

Open View details for the watch you like, or use Compare to review designs side by side. Before buying any watch, confirm the case dimensions, strap fit, movement and water resistance against verified manufacturer information.

What should I check before placing my order?

Review your chosen watch, quantity, delivery address and delivery method. If you have a discount code, apply it in your bag before checkout. Check the final total, including postage, then keep your order number from the confirmation screen.
